# ViralCut — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-21

ViralCut processes video locally on the user's computer. Some data leaves the device:

- **Audio for transcription** is sent to the Google Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com)
  to convert speech to text. Google's terms apply to that processing.
- **Video files** are uploaded to TikTok only when the user presses "Publish" and only to
  the TikTok account the user has connected.
- **TikTok access tokens** are stored locally in the user's profile folder
  (%APPDATA%\ViralCut) and are refreshed automatically.
- **API keys** (Gemini, TikTok) are stored locally in the application folder.

The application does not collect analytics, does not send data to the developer, and does
not share user content with any third party other than Google (transcription) and TikTok
(publishing), both initiated by the user.
