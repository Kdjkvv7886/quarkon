# ViralCut — Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026-11-21

ViralCut is a desktop application that cuts long videos into short vertical clips and
publishes them to TikTok through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

1. The application runs on the user's computer. The user is responsible for the content
   they process and publish.
2. Publishing to TikTok requires the user to connect their own TikTok account and to
   comply with TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
3. Speech recognition is performed by the Google Gemini API; by using the transcription
   feature the user agrees to send the audio track of their video to Google.
4. The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.
5. The user may stop using the application at any time and revoke TikTok access from
   their TikTok account settings.
